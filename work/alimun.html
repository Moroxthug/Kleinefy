The starting point.

Most language apps teach you to tap. People who need to speak, for a job, a move, an exam, stall after the first month because nobody is listening to them. The schools that do have teachers run on spreadsheets, a video link and a PDF course book sent by email.

Alimun began as our own answer: a school where every plan is built around a live class with a real teacher, in a fixed group on fixed days, and where everything between the classes, the book, the homework, the practice, lives in one place and talks to the teacher.

What we built.

We built it from nothing. The name. The voice. A cast of illustrated animals that students meet on the first page and keep meeting through the course. A website that explains the plans in one screen. And the platform under it: scheduling that puts a student in the right group at the right hour in their own time zone, the live video room, a course book per level, practice rounds, exam-style mock tests, spaced-repetition flashcards, a listening and reading library, and homework that comes back graded with strengths and what to improve.

Levels run from A1 to C2 in English and German, with more languages to come. The plans scale by group size: Community at about 35 people, Focused at about 17 with weekly feedback, Intensive at 6 with a one-to-one session a week, and 1 on 1. A corporate tier serves companies training teams.

The parts that matter.

  • A brand from zero.

    Name, voice, colour and a cast of illustrated animals that students recognise from the first lesson.

  • Live classes, scheduled.

    Fixed groups and fixed days, booked, reminded and joined from one screen, in the student’s time zone.

  • Homework, graded.

    Every exercise comes back with a grade, strengths and what to improve, visible to teacher and student.

  • An AI tutor for pronunciation.

    Students speak, the tutor listens and corrects, between the classes, so class time is for conversation.

  • A book per level.

    Course book, practice rounds, mock tests and flashcards built for each level, not bolted on.

  • Plans by group size.

    Community, Focused, Intensive and 1 on 1, plus a corporate tier, all on one platform.

Where it is now.

Alimun takes payments every week. Students join from several countries, classes run every week, and the illustrated world has become the thing people remember. The same system that schedules a class also bills for it and tells the teacher who did their homework.

What it proved for us: that we can carry a product from a blank page to a running school, and that the brand work is not decoration. People trust a place that looks like someone cared.
