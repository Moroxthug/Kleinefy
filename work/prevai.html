The starting point.

Italian artisans have the same evening as Canadian trades, with their own paperwork: preventivi, IVA at the right rate, contratti d’appalto, fatture, solleciti. Most of it still travels as a Word file and a phone call. The tools that exist are built for accountants, not for someone standing in a cantiere.

We had already built the engine. The question for prevAI was whether a product could cross a border without starting over: new language, new tax, new documents, new habits, same core.

What we built.

prevAI is quoteAI’s engine with an Italian heart. Describe the work in Italian, or send a vocale or a photo on WhatsApp, and get a preventivo with prices, logo, voci, quantità and IVA calculated, based on your listino and signed online by the client. Leads flow through a pipeline; an accepted preventivo becomes a cantiere with tasks, squad, suppliers and budget against actual; contratti d’appalto and fatture generate from it; solleciti go out until incasso.

Everything that is Italian was rebuilt rather than translated: numbering, tax, the documents themselves, the words on every button. Squadra, dashboard su fatturato e tasso di chiusura, assistente AI and import of existing listini and clients came with it.

How it works

The parts that matter.

  • IVA, built in.

    Rates and numbering handled in the document, with the preventivo and the fattura agreeing with each other.

  • Voice note to quote.

    An artisan describes the job out loud on WhatsApp and gets the preventivo back in the same chat.

  • Cantieri, contratti, fatture.

    Job sites, contracts and invoices in one flow, from the first contact to incasso.

  • Second market, same core.

    One codebase, two countries, two tax systems, two languages, one team maintaining it.

  • Squadra e ruoli.

    Multi-user accounts with roles and invites, so the office and the site see the same job.

  • Numbers you can read.

    Fatturato, tasso di chiusura and tempi di risposta on one dashboard, with an assistant that answers questions about them.

Where it is now.

prevAI runs in Italy with paying users, launched months rather than years after quoteAI, because the core was built to travel. For us it is the clearest proof of the way we work: build the thing properly once, and the second market, the second language and the second tax system become configuration, not a rewrite.

It is also why we say we go deepest with companies that run on process. The process is the product.
