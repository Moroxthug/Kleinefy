quoteAI. A priced, branded quote in 30 seconds, for Canadian trades.

The starting point.

A tradesperson loses the evening to quotes. Measurements in a notebook, prices in a spreadsheet from three years ago, a PDF built by hand, tax worked out province by province, then the chasing: did they get it, did they sign it, did they pay. The work is in the day; the business is in the night.

quoteAI started from one question: what if describing the job was enough? Say it in your own words, or send a photo or a voice note from the van, and get back a priced, branded quote with line items, quantities and the right GST, HST or PST for the province, in about thirty seconds.

What we built.

The quote is the front door, and we built the whole house behind it. Incoming leads land in a kanban pipeline before they become quotes. An accepted quote becomes a job site with tasks, deadlines, team assignment, suppliers and budget against actual. Contracts and invoices generate from the quote; clients get a public invoice page and reminders go out until it is paid. Every file, client and document stays searchable in one archive.

It is bilingual, English and French, and it works over WhatsApp: a message with a description or a voice note comes back as a quote. Teams get roles and invites, dashboards for revenue, win rate and turnaround, an AI assistant inside the dashboard, and imports for existing price lists and clients. A seven-day trial, no card.

How it works

The parts that matter.

  • 30 seconds to a quote.

    Plain words, a photo or a voice note in; a priced PDF with line items and quantities out, backed by your own price catalog.

  • Tax by province.

    GST, HST or GST plus PST, right for every province and territory, on every line.

  • Signed, invoiced, paid.

    E-signature on the quote, invoice generated from it, reminders on schedule until the money lands.

  • Works on WhatsApp.

    The app the trade already uses, in English or French, with a quote back in the same thread.

  • A quote becomes a job.

    Tasks, deadlines, team, suppliers and budget versus actual, linked to the original quote.

  • The office, in a dashboard.

    Revenue, win rate, turnaround, an AI assistant, multi-user roles and imports of what you already have.

Where it is now.

quoteAI runs across Canada with paying subscribers, and it is the product that taught us how trades actually run: from the first call to the deposit request, everything is a step that used to be retyped. The engine behind it was strong enough to launch in a second country as prevAI.

What it proved for us: that an idea with a clear job to do, built end to end, finds its users. And that the unglamorous parts, tax tables, reminders, imports, are what make people stay.
