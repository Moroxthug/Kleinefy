Online language school

B2C · EducationEnglish and German

We built Alimun from nothing: the name, the illustrated world, the website and the platform behind it, where students book live classes with a teacher, hand in homework that gets graded, drill flashcards and sit mock exams, from A1 to C2.

Role: Brand, product design, engineering, operations

A school that runs itself: scheduling, payments, homework and progress in one place.

Read the case

  • A brand from zero.

    Name, voice, colour and a cast of illustrated animals that students recognise from the first lesson.

  • Live classes, scheduled.

    Fixed groups and fixed days, booked, reminded and joined from one screen.

  • Homework, graded.

    Every exercise comes back with a grade, strengths and what to improve.

  • An AI tutor for pronunciation.

    Students speak, the tutor listens and corrects, between the classes.

Quoting and job platform

B2B SaaS · CanadaEnglish and French

Describe the job in your own words and quoteAI returns a priced, branded quote with line items and GST/HST by province. Then it runs the rest of the path: leads, contracts, invoices and payment, in English and French, even from WhatsApp.

Role: Product, design, engineering, go-to-market

Tradespeople quote from the truck and get paid without an office.

Read the case

  • 30 seconds to a quote.

    Plain words in, a priced PDF with line items and quantities out.

  • Tax by province.

    GST, HST or both, right for every province and territory, every time.

  • Signed, invoiced, paid.

    E-signature, invoice and payment reminders follow the quote without re-typing.

  • Works on WhatsApp.

    A message from the site becomes a quote, bilingual, in the app the trade already uses.

Preventivi e gestione lavori

B2B SaaS · ItalyItalian

prevAI takes the quoting engine to Italy: preventivi with IVA calculated, cantieri, contratti, fatture and incasso, and a quote generated from a single WhatsApp voice note. Same core, second market, second language, with its documents and tax done right.

Role: Product, design, engineering, localisation

A product launched in a second country without starting over.

Read the case

  • IVA, built in.

    Italian VAT rates and numbering handled in the document, not in someone's head.

  • Voice note to quote.

    An artisan describes the job out loud on WhatsApp and gets the preventivo back.

  • Cantieri, contratti, fatture.

    Job sites, contracts and invoices in one flow, from the first call to incasso.

  • Second market, same core.

    One codebase, two countries, two tax systems, two languages.
