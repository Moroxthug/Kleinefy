How a project runs.

Four phases, the same on every job, so you always know what is done, what is next and what it costs.

One

Survey

We walk your process end to end, find where time and money leak, and agree on what to build first.

Two

Design

Screens, data and integrations drawn and approved before any code ships. You see the product before you pay for it.

Three

Build

Working software in short cycles. Your team uses it early and steers it while changes are still cheap.

Four

Run

Training, documentation and support. You own the code and the data. We stay on call.

Questions, answered.

Do you build custom CRM and ERP systems for construction companies?

Yes. We design and build one system around how your company quotes, schedules, buys and invoices, and we connect it to the tools you already use, such as accounting and payroll.

Can you modernize our systems without stopping operations?

Yes. We replace spreadsheets, paper and old software one workflow at a time, running the new system beside the old one until your team is ready to switch.

How long does a project take?

A website usually takes three to six weeks. A CRM or ERP system ships its first working version in eight to twelve weeks and grows from there.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Websites, automations, CRM and ERP systems, mobile and web apps, and full products from idea to launch. We go deepest with companies that run on process, such as construction, trades and services, but a good idea from any industry is welcome.

Who owns the code?

You do. Everything we build for you is delivered with source code, documentation and the accounts in your name.

Tell us the idea.

A 30-minute call. You describe the website you want, the process that eats your week or the product in your head. We tell you what we would build, how long it takes and what it costs.
