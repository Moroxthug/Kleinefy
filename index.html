How a project runs.

Four phases, the same on every job, so you always know what is done, what is next and what it costs.

One

Survey

We walk your business end to end, find where time and money leak, and agree on what to build first.

Two

Design

Screens, data and integrations drawn and approved before any code ships. You see the product before you pay for it.

Three

Build

Working software in short cycles. Your team uses it early and steers it while changes are still cheap.

Four

Run

Launch, search visibility, training and support. You own the code and the data. We stay on call.

No templates. No page builders. No agency handing your project to a freelancer. Kleinefy is a small team in Vancouver that designs, writes and ships every line itself. We answer the phone, we argue for the better idea, and we are accountable for the result.

Questions, answered.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Websites, SEO and GEO, automations, CRM and ERP systems, mobile and web apps, and full products from idea to launch. We go deepest with companies that run on process, but a good idea from any industry is welcome.

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimization. More and more people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI instead of scrolling results. We structure your content, data and reputation so those assistants find you, trust you and name you as the answer.

Do you build custom CRM and ERP systems?

Yes. We design and build one system around how your company sells, delivers and bills, and we connect it to the tools you already use, such as accounting and payroll.

How long does a project take?

A website usually takes three to six weeks. A CRM or ERP system ships its first working version in eight to twelve weeks and grows from there.

Who owns the code?

You do. Everything we build for you is delivered with source code, documentation and the accounts in your name.

Tell us the idea.

A 30-minute call. You describe the website you want, the process that eats your week or the product in your head. We tell you what we would build, how long it takes and what it costs.
